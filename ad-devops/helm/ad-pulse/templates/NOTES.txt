Ad Pulse is installed in the namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Check that the services answer from inside the cluster:
  helm test {{ .Release.Name }} --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}

The manager, the dashboard, the ad server and the engagement service are LoadBalancer Services. Their addresses can take a minute to appear:
  kubectl get svc --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}
